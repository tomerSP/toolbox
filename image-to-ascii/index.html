Image to ASCII art converter

Turn your image into monochrome ASCII artwork. Compare, then download reusable text.

Processing stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Browser-supported raster images, up to 20 MB and 16 million pixels. Full image, usually 80 columns; extreme shapes use fewer columns or at most 240 rows. Animated images use a single frame.

Choose an image to get started.
