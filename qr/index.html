Why can't I put a big file in a QR code?

A QR code is not storage — it is a printed string of at most 2,953 bytes, and that is the absolute ceiling at the lowest error-correction level. Files are encoded here as a data: URI, which costs about a third extra on top of the raw size, so the practical limit is roughly 2 KB. Anything larger has to be uploaded somewhere and linked to by URL.
